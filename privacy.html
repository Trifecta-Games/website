Privacy Policy

Effective: January 1, 2025 Last updated: May 2026

At Trifecta Studios, we build games people enjoy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our mobile games and applications. We are committed to being transparent about our practices and protecting the privacy of our players.

01

Who We Are

Trifecta Studios is an independent mobile game development studio. We develop and publish mobile games available on platforms including Google Play and the Apple App Store. This policy applies to all games and applications published by Trifecta Studios.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on the game and the services integrated within it, we may collect or receive the following types of information:

  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Gameplay data including progress, scores, session duration, and in-game actions
  • Technical data such as crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) where applicable
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, where relevant to service delivery
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting our support team

We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information unless you provide them directly to us.

03

How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely to operate, maintain, and improve our games and services. Specifically, we may use it to:

  • Deliver and improve gameplay experience
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues and crashes
  • Analyse gameplay trends to improve game design
  • Deliver relevant advertisements where our games are ad-supported
  • Comply with legal obligations
04

Third-Party Services

Our games may integrate third-party SDKs and services for purposes such as analytics, advertising, and crash reporting. These third parties may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We take reasonable steps to ensure that any third-party partners we work with uphold appropriate privacy standards.

Third-party services we may use include, but are not limited to, analytics providers, advertising networks, and platform services provided by Google and Apple. Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

05

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

06

Data Retention & Security

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

07

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

You may also opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings by resetting or limiting your advertising identifier.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of our games after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

09

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to us.
