About the Project

Master Sab is a pioneering EdTech platform built to make science education accessible and engaging for Bangladeshi students at the SSC, HSC, and undergraduate levels. Instead of relying on rote memorization, the platform uses 3D visualizations and real-time simulations to bring biology, chemistry, and physics to life.

Trifecta Studios contributed to the platform's interactive 3D experiences and simulation modules, bringing game development craft into the education space.

Key Features

  • 3D Visualization: rotate and explore cells, molecules, and organs in full 3D
  • Interactive Simulations: chemical reactions, physics principles, and biological processes in real time
  • AI-Powered Problem Solving: intelligent assistance for tackling scientific challenges
  • MCQ Assessments: built-in quiz tools for evaluation and exam prep
  • Multi-Platform: available on smartphones, desktops, and smart boards
